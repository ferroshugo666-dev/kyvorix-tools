Contact Kyvorix

Have a question, found a problem or want to suggest a new tool? We would be happy to hear from you.

Get in touch

For questions, feedback, bug reports or suggestions, please contact the Kyvorix team by email at contact@kyvorixapp.com.

Important: Please do not send passwords, payment card information, identity documents or other highly sensitive personal information through a general contact message.

Bug reports

When reporting a problem, it is helpful to include the name of the Kyvorix tool, the type of file you were using, your browser and a short description of what happened.

Suggestions

If there is a tool or feature you would like to see on Kyvorix, feel free to send a suggestion. User feedback helps determine which improvements should be prioritized.

Privacy questions

For questions specifically related to personal data or privacy, please reference the Privacy Policy.
