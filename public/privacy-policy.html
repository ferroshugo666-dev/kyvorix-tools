Privacy Policy

Kyvorix provides online tools designed to process files and content in a simple, fast and privacy-conscious way. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the Kyvorix website.

Last updated: September 6, 2026

1. Information we collect

Kyvorix tools are designed so that supported file processing can take place directly in your browser. When a tool operates locally, the selected file is not intentionally uploaded to a Kyvorix server for processing.

We may collect limited technical information necessary to operate, secure and improve the website, such as browser type, device information, approximate location derived from IP address, pages visited and basic usage information.

2. Local file processing

Many Kyvorix tools process files directly in your browser using client-side technology. This means that, where indicated by the individual tool, your original file can remain on your device while the browser performs the requested conversion, compression, resizing or other operation.

3. Analytics

Kyvorix may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website, which pages are useful and how the service can be improved.

Analytics information may include pages viewed, approximate geographic information, device information, browser information and interactions with the website.

4. Advertising

Kyvorix uses Google AdSense to display advertisements provided by Google and may use cookies or similar technologies for advertising, measurement and personalization, subject to applicable laws and your consent choices.

Google may use information about visits to this website and other websites to provide and measure ads. You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services at Google's partner-sites policy.

5. Cookies and consent

Kyvorix may use cookies and similar technologies for essential functionality, analytics and advertising.

Where legally required, visitors will be presented with appropriate consent choices before non-essential cookies or personalized advertising technologies are used.

6. Third-party services

The website may use third-party services such as analytics, advertising, content delivery or hosting providers. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

7. Data retention

Kyvorix does not intentionally retain uploaded files when a tool processes them locally in the browser. Information processed by third-party services may be retained according to the policies and settings of those services.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to the processing of your personal data. You may also have the right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

9. Children

Kyvorix is not specifically directed at children under the age required by applicable law, and we do not knowingly request personal information from children for the purpose of creating accounts.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Kyvorix, please use the Contact page.
