Why convert WebP to PNG?

WebP is designed for efficient web delivery, while PNG is widely supported for graphics, screenshots, editing workflows and images that require transparency.

Converting WebP to PNG can be useful when another application, editor or workflow specifically requires a PNG file.

PNG uses lossless compression, but converting a lossy WebP image to PNG cannot restore image detail that was already lost in the original WebP.

WebP to PNG Converter FAQ

Can I convert WebP to PNG for free?

Yes. Kyvorix provides a free WebP to PNG converter that works directly in your browser.

Are my WebP files uploaded?

No. The conversion is performed directly in your browser, so the WebP image does not need to be uploaded to a Kyvorix server.

Does WebP to PNG preserve transparency?

Yes. PNG supports transparency, so transparent areas in a WebP image can be preserved when the image is decoded by the browser.

Can I convert an animated WebP?

This converter processes a WebP as a still image. Animated WebP files are not converted as an animation sequence.
