Frequently asked questions

Can I crop images without uploading them?

Yes. Processing happens locally in your browser.

Which formats are supported?

JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP are supported.

Can I crop to a fixed ratio?

Yes. Choose 1:1, 4:5, 16:9 or 3:2, or select Free for unrestricted cropping.
