Why convert HEIC to JPG?

HEIC and HEIF are modern image formats commonly used for efficient photo storage, particularly in Apple device workflows.

JPG is supported by a very wide range of websites, applications and devices, making it useful when a service does not accept HEIC files.

Converting a HEIC photo to JPG creates a new image file. Compression and metadata may therefore differ from the original HEIC file.
