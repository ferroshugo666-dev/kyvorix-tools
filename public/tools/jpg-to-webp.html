Why convert JPG to WebP?

WebP is an image format designed for modern websites. Depending on the image and quality setting, WebP can provide smaller files than JPEG while maintaining good visual quality.

Converting JPG images to WebP can therefore be useful when optimizing images for websites, online stores, blogs and other web-based applications.
