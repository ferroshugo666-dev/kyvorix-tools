How to convert WebP to JPG

Select a WebP image or drag it into the converter above. Choose your preferred JPG quality and click “Convert to JPG”.

After conversion, preview the result and download your JPG file.

Why convert WebP to JPG?

JPG is still widely supported by image editors, websites, applications and older systems. Converting WebP to JPG can be useful when a platform does not accept WebP files.

Keep in mind that JPG does not support transparency. Transparent parts of a WebP image are therefore converted to a white background.

Frequently asked questions

Is this WebP to JPG converter free?

Yes. The tool is free to use in your browser.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

The conversion is performed directly in your browser. Your selected image does not need to be uploaded to a remote server.

Can I control JPG quality?

Yes. Use the quality slider before converting the image. Higher quality generally produces a larger file.

Does JPG support transparency?

No. Transparent areas are rendered against a white background when converting to JPG.
