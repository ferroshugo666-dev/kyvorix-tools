Why convert PNG to JPG?

JPG is commonly used for photographs and web images because JPEG compression can produce substantially smaller files than uncompressed or lossless formats in many situations.

Converting a PNG to JPG can therefore be useful when you need a smaller image file or when a service, website, or application specifically requires JPG.

Keep in mind that JPG uses lossy compression. Converting a PNG to JPG can reduce image quality, especially at lower quality settings.

PNG vs JPG
