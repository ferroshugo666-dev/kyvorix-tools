How to resize an image

Resize an image to custom dimensions or use one of the available presets.

1

Choose your image

Select a JPG, PNG or WebP image from your device, or drag it into the tool.

2

Enter the width and height you need or select a preset. Keep aspect ratio enabled to preserve the original proportions.

3

Resize and download

Image Resizer FAQ

Can I resize images for free?

Yes. Kyvorix provides a free image resizer that works directly in your browser.

Which image formats are supported?

The resizer accepts JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP images.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Resizing is performed directly in your browser, so your image does not need to be uploaded to a Kyvorix server.

Can I keep the original proportions?

Yes. Enable Keep aspect ratio and changing one dimension will automatically calculate the other.
