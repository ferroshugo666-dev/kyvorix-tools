Why convert HEIC to JPG?

HEIC and HEIF are commonly used for efficient photo storage, particularly in Apple device workflows. JPG is a widely recognized format that can be easier to use with older applications, websites and workflows.

Converting a HEIC photo to JPG can therefore be useful when an application or website does not accept the original HEIC format.

Conversion may change image compression and metadata. The converted JPG should be treated as a new image file, not an exact container-level copy of the original.
