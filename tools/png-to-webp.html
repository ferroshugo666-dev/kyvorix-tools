How to convert PNG to WebP

Select a PNG image or drag it into the converter. Choose a WebP quality level and click “Convert to WebP”.

When the conversion finishes, preview the WebP image and download it to your device.

Why convert PNG to WebP?

WebP can provide smaller image files than PNG in many common use cases, which can be useful for websites and other digital projects.

This converter keeps the transparent background of PNG images because the WebP format supports transparency.

PNG to WebP Converter FAQ
