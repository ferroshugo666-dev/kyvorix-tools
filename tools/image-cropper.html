How to crop an image

1

Choose your image

Select a JPG, PNG or WebP image from your device, or drag it into the tool.

2

Choose the crop ratio

Use Free crop for a custom selection or choose a fixed ratio such as 1:1, 4:5, 16:9 or 3:2.

3

Crop and download

Adjust the crop area, click Crop image, and download the resulting image.

Common image crop ratios

1:1 Square images and profile-style layouts.
4:5 Tall portrait layouts commonly used for social content.
16:9 Widescreen layouts and video thumbnails.
3:2 A common photographic aspect ratio.

Image Cropper FAQ

Can I crop images for free?

Yes. Kyvorix provides a free image cropper that works directly in your browser.

Which image formats are supported?

The cropper accepts JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP images.

Can I crop to a specific aspect ratio?

Yes. You can use free cropping or choose 1:1, 4:5, 16:9 or 3:2.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Image processing is performed directly in your browser, so the image does not need to be uploaded to a Kyvorix server.
